## What this is

Core-tier constants encoded exactly from the P130 prompt:

- **Bedoya-Maya, F., Shobayo, P., Beckers, J., van Hassel, E. (2024)**,
  *Transportation Research Part D*, DOI: 10.1016/j.trd.2024.104190
  - average throughput disturbance **-0.2% per day** under critical
    water level
  - **-5.9%** when disruption lasts over 24 days
  - vulnerability to critical conditions **doubled since 2018**
  - without resilience measures: **7-20%** projected annual container
    throughput loss by 2050
- **Vinke, F.R.S. et al. (2022)**, *Climate Risk Management* 35, 100400,
  DOI: 10.1016/j.crm.2022.100400 -- systemic cascade / network-effect
  model (Rhine 2018 case). Encoded as cascade-mechanics note, **not**
  as a single threshold constant.
- **Kaub gauge (Rhine), 78 cm** -- documented, cross-confirmed
  operational consensus (ICIS, Chemistry World, freight trade press),
  not single-study-based. Under severe events load factor falls to
  **10-30%** of nominal capacity.
- **IfW Kiel (2018)**, institute statement on kielinstitut.de: on an
  annual view low water cost about **0.4%** of economic output;
  methodologically linked to industrial production decline of ~**1%**
  (peak **1.5%**) at **30 days** below 78 cm Kaub.
